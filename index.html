Find out how the intended digital store and membership experience will work.

Navyukti is a planned digital solutions brand offering AI resources, digital products, business toolkits, and productivity resources.
Planned categories include AI prompts, workflow templates, productivity planners, content resources, business templates, and curated digital bundles. Actual products will be listed as they become available.
The planned store will provide purchased resources through a configured digital delivery system. Secure download delivery is not enabled in this frontend prototype.
The planned experience supports guest checkout alongside optional customer accounts. Account functionality must be implemented before launch.
The intended payment options include UPI, cards, net banking, and eligible international payments. Availability depends on the selected payment provider, account approval, and configuration.
Membership cancellation terms and renewal behavior must be published before subscriptions are offered. The live service will need a working cancellation process and subscription management.
The intended policy will explain how duplicate payments, failed delivery, verified technical issues, and eligible cancellation requests are handled. The final policy must be published and comply with applicable consumer protection laws.
